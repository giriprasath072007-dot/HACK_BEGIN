ABOUT THIS WORKSPACE

Privacy & processing

A transparent foundation for responsible document review.

Your documents remain in this browser

This frontend demo does not transmit your files to a server, scan for malware, extract their text, or perform real AI analysis. File names and metadata are held in page memory only and are cleared when you refresh or close the tab.

For production use, connect an authenticated backend with file-type and size validation, malware scanning, protected storage, clear retention controls, and explicit deletion. Never present generated source confidence or credibility assessments as established facts.
